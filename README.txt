DEADLY ASSAULT PROTOTYPE (Godot 4.3+, Forward+ renderer)
Open project.godot, let the editor import art/ and audio/, press F5, then ENTER.

WASD move | J / Click attack | K / Shift dodge | L EX | Q/E or 1/2/3 swap
Space ultimate | F chain attack (when the boss is stunned) | ESC pause | R restart

Tuning:  scripts/GameConfig.gd   balance, camera, USE_3D_MODELS, MUSIC_DB, SFX_DB
         scripts/Model3D.gd      3D models + spring-physics values (hair, tails, skirts, weapons)
         scripts/Sfx.gd          synthesised sound effects + music loader (audio/bgm.mp3)
